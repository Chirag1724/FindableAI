Our Story

Founded in early 2026, Bistro 2026 was conceived as an intimate sanctuary where modern culinary technique honors age-old agrarian traditions. Every ingredient is ethically harvested and prepared with obsessive attention to purity and flavor.

Executive Chef Hélène Laurent brings two decades of Michelin-starred expertise to curate a living menu that responds directly to regional micro-seasons.
